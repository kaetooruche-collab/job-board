Reply templates

The order and service emails, in the same look as our Brevo emails, ready to paste into Gmail. Pick one, type the name and package, change anything else right in the preview, then copy. This page is unlisted and not indexed, and it keeps nothing you type.

Yellow means "still to fill in". Click into the preview and type over it, or delete a paragraph you don't need. The yellow never reaches Gmail.

In Gmail, every time

  1. In yirehcareers@gmail.com, open the client's email thread and click Reply (or Compose for a new email).
  2. Here: choose the template, fill in the name and package, edit the rest, tap Copy email.
  3. In Gmail, click in the message body and paste (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac). Then tap Copy subject here and paste it into the subject (leave the subject alone when replying in a thread).
  4. Attach any files. Read it once, then send.

If a template is also in your Gmail "Templates", ignore that copy: this page is the up-to-date one.

Check before the first real send:
  • Send yourself one first (from this page to kaetooruche@gmail.com) and open it on your phone. Pasted logos and layout can look different in each mail app. If the logo doesn't show, untick "Include the logo".
  • Service emails carry no offers, prices or promotion. Don't add any (the only prices are the ones already in the templates).
  • Never paste a CV or bank details into this page. It doesn't need them.
