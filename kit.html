Posting rules that keep the accounts safe and the posts trusted
  • Read each group's rules first. Many groups ban links; ask the admin or post as text with the link in a comment.
  • One post per group per day. Posting the same text into many groups at once gets accounts flagged as spam.
  • Never add "guaranteed", "no work permit needed" or claims about who a job suits. The posts already tell people to check each employer's eligibility rules.
  • Reply to every comment and message. Correct anything wrong in public the same day (trust rule 6).
  • Check the link opens before you post it (the page shows the live address).
