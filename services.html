Meet the person behind Yireh Careers

Kaeto Oruche · Founder

I wanted remote work that would fit around my life, so I applied to over 200 remote jobs. Every time I found a role that looked right, the real work started: researching who had posted it, picking the posting apart, writing a CV, finding out it wasn’t built for applicant tracking systems, and learning that a cover letter only works when it genuinely speaks to that job. It took so much time, for every application.

I started Yireh Careers to fix that. The job board links every role to the employer’s own posting and shows who can apply. The CV, cover letter and LinkedIn help is the support I wished I’d had.

It is a positioning strategy: how you should be presented for the roles you want, and which kinds of people you should be connected with, such as recruiters, hiring managers, experienced professionals in your field, peers and industry specialists. It is more than a headline or a profile photo.
