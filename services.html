Inside the Executive package

More than career documents.

Executive combines professional presentation, deliberate network building and ongoing LinkedIn content. Here is exactly what each part means.

Personal branding strategy

This is not just a LinkedIn headline or a nicer profile photo. We work out how you should be positioned for the roles you want, and which kinds of people you should be connected with to support your career goals.

Depending on your background, industry and objectives, that can include:

  • Recruiters and talent acquisition professionals
  • Hiring managers and other decision-makers
  • Experienced professionals in your target field
  • Peers and communities relevant to you
  • Industry specialists

The aim is relevant professional relationships, not a random pile of connections. We cannot promise that any connection leads to a reply, an interview or a job offer.

Manual, targeted connection building

We choose the people to connect with based on your target industry, desired roles, existing experience and expertise, professional positioning and career objectives.

Our target is 500+ connections in total, counting the connections you already have. The 30 days are the initial service window, not a fixed end date. If you have not reached 500+ by day 30, connection building continues until you do, subject to the agreed scope and your continued cooperation.

How long it takes depends on where you start: a profile beginning with 24 connections and one beginning with 134 will not take the same time. Growth stays manual and targeted, prioritising relevant professional connections over indiscriminate requests. We cannot promise how many people accept or how they respond.

LinkedIn content, written and published

We write and publish 12 curated posts for you over the 30 days, about 3 a week, tailored to your background, target industry, career goals and branding strategy.

The posts reflect your real experience and knowledge. We do not invent achievements or opinions. This is an active service: we produce and publish the content, not just advise you on what to post. Posts go out across the month, not all at once.

The purpose is to strengthen your professional identity and keep a consistent presence. We do not guarantee impressions, followers, recruiter replies or interviews.

The Executive timeline

Everything below is counted from when your onboarding is complete. Onboarding means you have sent us your current CV and the roles you are targeting, and answered our questions about your goals.

  • CV and cover letter: delivered together within 48 hours (2 working days).
  • LinkedIn profile setup: delivered within 5 working days.
  • Personal branding and connection building: delivered over the initial 30-day service period, together with the 12 posts.

The 30 days are the initial window, not a fixed end date. If you have not reached 500+ total connections by day 30, connection building continues until you do.

Dates can move if we are waiting for your information, feedback or approvals. If a delivery date changes materially, we will tell you.
