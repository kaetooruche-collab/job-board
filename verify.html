How we verify listings

Every listing on Yireh Careers goes through a verification process before it appears. We built this board for job seekers who are tired of broken links, vague descriptions and roles that don't exist. Here is exactly what we check.

What “verified” means here

We use the word for specific checks, not as a blanket promise. Here is exactly what is and is not checked.

What we check, on every update (every 6 hours)

  • The listing comes from the employer’s own job feed, and its apply link goes to that posting, never a homepage or a search page.
  • The link opens. A listing whose link fails is removed.
  • The listing is still in the employer’s feed and was posted within the last 30 days (by its original posting date).
  • The whole posting reads as remote. Hybrid, on-site and self-contradicting roles are left out.
  • “Who can apply” shows what the employer states, or “unclear” when they do not say.
  • Pay is shown only when the posting states it.

What we do not check

  • We do not investigate each employer. We choose which employers to include, but we do not audit them.
  • We cannot see inside an employer’s hiring process. A role can occasionally be filled before the employer removes it from their feed.
  • We do not confirm that an applicant from your country will be accepted. That is the employer’s decision, so read their eligibility rules before you apply.
  • We do not promise interviews or job offers.

Our trust rules

1
We never charge a job seeker to apply or to see a listing's apply link.
2
We never post a listing that asks applicants to pay.
3
Commission-only roles are excluded.
4
Every listing shows its source, posting date and when we last checked it.
5
Every claim we publish links to the posting that supports it.
6
A wrong listing is corrected in public within one day.

What we check before publishing

Direct link to the posting

Every listing links to the employer's actual job posting on their applicant tracking system or the original source. We never link to a homepage, search page or category page.

Original posting date

The posted date comes from the source, using the original posting date rather than the last edit. Anything older than 30 days is automatically removed.

Who can apply

Remote does not always mean worldwide. We read where the employer says applicants must live or be authorised to work and show it on every card: “Worldwide”, a named country or region, or “Eligibility unclear” when the employer does not say. We never assume a role is open worldwide. Check the employer's rules for your location before applying.

Pay verification

Pay is shown only when the employer states it in the posting. We never invent or estimate figures. If pay is not stated, we show "Not stated".

Link check

Every listing's link is checked at each update. Listings are read from the employer's own job feed, which only lists open roles. A listing is removed when its link fails, redirects to an error page, or disappears from the feed.

Is it really remote?

We read the whole posting, not just the title or location field. Hybrid roles and roles that need regular office attendance are left out, and so are roles that contradict themselves (they are sent for a manual look). Roles marketed as remote that do not say clearly whether they are fully remote are kept, but are labelled “Remote: not confirmed” with the reason. Every card has a “Why this label?” note showing the wording we relied on and what we could not confirm.

Every experience level

Listings range from internships and entry-level roles to senior and leadership positions across many fields. We work out the level from the experience the posting asks for, not only from words like “junior” or “senior” in the title, and you can filter by it.

A listing is published only if all of these hold

The checks above cover the link, the date, the remote status, who can apply and the pay. On top of those, a listing goes up only when:

  • The source is credited and linked, and the page shows no closed-role wording.
  • It is not commission-only and charges no applicant fee.
  • It is not older than 30 days.
  • None of the checks above came back with a problem.

See something wrong?

If a listing is incorrect, expired or suspicious, let us know and we will fix it within one day.
