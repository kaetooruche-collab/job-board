How we verify listings

  • We never charge a job seeker to apply or to see a listing's apply link.
  • We never post a listing that asks applicants to pay.
  • Commission-only roles are excluded.
  • Every listing shows its source, posting date and when we last checked it.
  • Every claim we publish links to the posting that supports it.
  • A wrong listing is corrected in public within one day.
