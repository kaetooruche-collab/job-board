  &lt;strong&gt;Headquarters:&lt;/strong&gt; Poland - Remote

&lt;div class="content-intro"&gt;&lt;div&gt;Wrike is the most powerful work management platform. Built for teams and organizations looking to collaborate, create, and exceed every day, Wrike brings everyone and all work into a single place to remove complexity, increase productivity, and free people up to focus on their most purposeful work.&lt;/div&gt;
&lt;div&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/div&gt;
&lt;div&gt;Wrike is our people, not a place. As a distributed team, we own our growth, stay globally connected, and rely on the product we build to deliver impactful work alongside brilliant minds. You'll have real ownership over meaningful work, a global team that has your back, and the flexibility to do your best work your way. If that sounds like you, we'd love to hear from you.&lt;/div&gt;
&lt;div&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/div&gt;
&lt;div&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Our vision:&amp;nbsp; &lt;/strong&gt;A world where everyone is free to focus on their most purposeful work, together.&amp;nbsp;&lt;/div&gt;
&lt;div&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/div&gt;&lt;/div&gt;&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;About the Role:&amp;nbsp;&lt;/strong&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;You'll help build the very AI-powered platform you use every day, with real autonomy over the features you ship. Wrike’s Backend Reliability (BRE) team is the backbone of our backend infrastructure and the guardian of our uptime. Our mission is to achieve and sustain 99.99% availability while building the tools, components, and safety nets that the entire engineering organization relies on. As a Senior / Staff Backend Engineer on this team, you won’t just close tickets - you’ll architect core reliability solutions that shape how Wrike scales, performs, and recovers from failure.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Your Impact:&amp;nbsp;&lt;/strong&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Design, build, and maintain critical reliability components such as HTTP rate limiters, internal DB schema migration tools, circuit breakers, and distributed Redis-based caching.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Troubleshoot complex production issues, optimize PostgreSQL usage, and ensure our distributed systems remain performant and stable under high load.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Lead preliminary investigations during severe production incidents: identify likely root causes, assess impact, and propose mitigation options. The long-term fixes are then implemented by the owning team, based on your findings.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Create scalable, reusable tools and frameworks that help other engineering teams build more resilient services.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Leverage AI-powered tools and coding agents to accelerate development, analyze architectures, and automate repetitive or error-prone tasks.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Influence reliability best practices across engineering by sharing knowledge, reviewing designs, and setting high technical standards.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Your Qualifications:&amp;nbsp;&lt;/strong&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Strong expertise with Java/JVM, building scalable, high-performance backend systems; open to leveraging other languages when appropriate.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Solid understanding of distributed systems concepts, including high availability, CAP theorem, and fault tolerance.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Deep experience with relational databases (PostgreSQL) and key–value / non-relational storages (Redis).&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Practical experience with containerization and cloud-native environments, including Docker and Kubernetes.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Hands-on experience with message brokers such as RabbitMQ or Kafka.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Ability to work independently with minimal supervision, using critical thinking to question assumptions and validate your own decisions.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Strong written and spoken English skills suitable for collaborating in an international engineering environment.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Standout Qualities:&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Background in infrastructure engineering or Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), including infrastructure-as-code practices.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Experience leading technical initiatives, driving cross-team projects, and mentoring other engineers while remaining an individual contributor.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Familiarity with observability and monitoring stacks (e.g., Graylog, Zabbix, Grafana) and/or data analytics tools such as BigQuery.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;A strong interest in how complex systems fail and a track record of designing them to recover gracefully.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Team Dynamics:&amp;nbsp;&lt;/strong&gt;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;You will join the Backend Reliability (BRE) team, a small, highly specialized, senior group focused solely on Wrike’s reliability. The team operates as an internal “reliability task force,” partnering closely with product and platform engineering teams across the company. You’ll collaborate with other senior engineers who value autonomy, deep technical discussions, and rigorous engineering practices. The culture is ownership-driven: you are trusted to manage your time, make architectural decisions, and drive initiatives to completion.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;h3&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Our Work Style:&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/h3&gt;
&lt;p&gt;The BRE team works on core backend and infrastructure services that support millions of users. We operate in a collaborative environment that values clear communication, thoughtful design, and fast feedback loops.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Tech focus areas include: Java/JVM-based services, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, Kubernetes, RabbitMQ/Kafka, and observability/monitoring tools.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;We encourage the use of modern AI-based tooling and coding agents as part of daily development and troubleshooting workflows.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Work is organized with an emphasis on impact and reliability goals rather than ticket volume, giving you room for deep work and long-term improvements.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Hybrid work mode (Prague, Czech Republic / Nicosia, Cyprus), with flexibility to balance focused individual work and collaborative sessions.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;

&lt;div class="content-intro"&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Why join us&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Brex is the intelligent finance platform that enables companies to spend smarter and move faster in more than 200 markets. By combining global corporate cards and banking with intuitive spend management, bill pay, and travel software, Brex enables founders and finance teams to accelerate operations, gain real-time visibility, and control spend effortlessly. Brex’s AI-native automation and world-class service eliminate manual expense and accounting tasks for customers so they can focus on what matters most. Tens of thousands of the world's best companies run on Brex, including DoorDash, Coinbase, Robinhood, Zoom, Plaid, Reddit, and SeatGeek.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Working at Brex allows you to push your limits, challenge the status quo, and collaborate with some of the brightest minds in the industry. We’re committed to building a diverse team and inclusive culture and believe your potential should only be limited by how big you can dream. We make this a reality by empowering you with the tools, resources, and support you need to grow your career.&lt;/p&gt;&lt;/div&gt;&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Marketing at Brex&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Marketing tells the story of Brex to the world. From acquisition to activation, we translate product value into business results. Our team spans Revenue, Product, and Brand Marketing, and works closely with nearly every function at Brex. We move fast, experiment often, and think deeply about customer behavior. If you want your creativity to drive growth and shape perception, this is the place.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What You'll Do&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;As a Field Marketing Coordinator, Upmarket, you'll play an important role in helping Brex scale regional marketing programs that drive pipeline generation, accelerate opportunities, and strengthen relationships with senior finance, accounting, and procurement leaders across Enterprise and Mid-Market organizations.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;Reporting directly to the Field Marketing Lead, Upmarket, you'll support a diverse portfolio of revenue-driving initiatives, including executive experiences, regional events, account-based marketing (ABM), and digital campaigns.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;You'll take ownership of day-to-day program execution and operational coordination, working closely with Sales, Marketing, and cross-functional partners to bring programs to life. Your work will help expand our regional presence, improve program efficiency, and ensure consistent Sales activation before, during, and after each program.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;The ideal candidate is a proactive, highly organized marketer who thrives in a fast-paced environment, builds strong relationships with Sales, and understands how thoughtful execution drives business growth. You’ll bring attention to detail, creative problem-solving skills, and the ability to balance multiple projects and competing priorities while delivering high-quality work.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Where you'll work&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;This role will be fully remote and based in the United States.&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Responsibilities&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Own day-to-day planning and execution for Upmarket field marketing programs, including executive experiences, regional dinners, sporting events, and digital activations.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Coordinate event logistics, including venue sourcing, vendor management, catering, gifting, shipping, registration, attendee communications, budgets, and onsite execution.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Develop and maintain event timelines, registration pages, attendee trackers, run-of-show documents, and internal event briefs.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Partner with AEs, SDRs, and Sales leadership to support account targeting, prospect outreach, attendee engagement, and pre- and post-event follow-up.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Execute smaller, repeatable regional programs and sales-led experiences that strengthen customer relationships, support multi-threading, and accelerate pipeline.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Support integrated digital marketing campaigns across email and paid media, coordinating audience lists, creative assets, landing pages, campaign launches, and performance tracking.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Coordinate ABM and direct-mail campaigns, including prospect targeting, vendor management, gifting, fulfillment, and delivery tracking.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Maintain the Field Marketing events calendar, campaign documentation, and performance reporting across Salesforce, registration platforms, and marketing automation tools, tracking meetings generated, qualified pipeline, and pipeline influence.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Requirements&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;2+ years of experience in field marketing, event marketing, demand generation, project management or a related role.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Strong project management and organizational skills, with the ability to manage multiple projects, deadlines, and stakeholders simultaneously.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work cross-functionally.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Proactive, resourceful, and comfortable taking ownership of day-to-day execution with minimal supervision.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Ability to adapt to changing priorities, solve problems quickly, and maintain high-quality execution under tight deadlines.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;Ability to travel up to 40% of the time to support regional events and onsite execution.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;/ul&gt;
&lt;p&gt;&lt;strong&gt;What Success Looks Like&lt;/strong&gt;&lt;/p&gt;
&lt;ul&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Seamless program execution:&lt;/strong&gt; Deliver high-quality field events and integrated campaigns on time, within budget, and with minimal operational friction.&lt;/li&gt;
&lt;li&gt;&lt;strong&gt;Increased program capacity and efficiency:&lt;/strong&gt; Expand the team's ability to execute more regional and digital programs through repeatable processes, automation, and streamlined workflows.&lt;/li&gt;
