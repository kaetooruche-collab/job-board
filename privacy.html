Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it, and how you can ask for it, correct it or have it deleted.

In short

  • We use your CV and personal information only to deliver the services you bought, to give you a free quick CV check if you ask for one, and to support you with them.
  • We do not sell your information and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.
  • Working files and personal information are deleted within 90 days after your service is completed. Provider backup copies are removed on the provider's own schedule.
  • You can ask for your information, a correction or a deletion at any time. We reply within one month, free of charge.
  • Please send documents by email, not WhatsApp. Email is not end-to-end encrypted, so send only what is needed for your CV or profile, and never passport or ID numbers, bank details or passwords.

Who we are

Yireh Careers is run by an individual business owner based in Kyrenia, Northern Cyprus. It is not a registered company. Privacy contact: yirehcareers@gmail.com.

You choose what goes in your CV. CVs sometimes contain sensitive details such as a photo, date of birth, health, religion or ethnicity. You do not need to send these to us. If your CV contains them, we use them only to deliver your service and delete them with your other files.

Who we share it with

Only the people and providers needed to do the work. We do not sell your information. Providers act for us and we use their services as follows:

  • Whop (our card payment provider for Essential and Professional). When you pay by card you enter your card details on a checkout page hosted by Whop, not on this website. Whop handles them under its own privacy policy and tells us what you bought and whether the payment succeeded.
  • Your bank and other payment providers you choose (for bank transfer we see the payer name, amount, currency and reference). If you pay in cryptocurrency, the blockchain network records the transaction publicly and permanently; we cannot erase it.
  • Brevo (the weekly job email, tips and offers, and the notices our forms send to our inbox). Brevo records whether emails are opened and which links are clicked.
  • Gmail / Google (our email) and WhatsApp / Meta (messages with you).
  • Vercel (hosts this website and runs the small program that passes your form details to Brevo; it keeps server logs that include visitors' IP addresses) and GitHub (stores the website code; no client files are kept there).
  • GoatCounter (website statistics): it sets no cookies and stores no IP address.
  • AI writing tools. We use AI writing tools only to help improve the wording of documents. Text from your documents may be entered into these tools, which are run by other companies and may be located outside your country. They do not make decisions about you, and we check everything before it is sent to you.

Sending information abroad

Some of these providers are in the United States or other countries. Where information is sent outside your country, we rely on the legal safeguards the providers use, such as adequacy decisions, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. You can ask us for details of the safeguards for a provider.

How we protect it

  • Documents are exchanged by email only; WhatsApp is used for conversation, not documents.
  • Our email account is protected by two-step sign-in, and we keep each client's files in one labelled place and delete them after completion.
  • Access is limited to what is needed to do the work.
  • We keep a record of when each client's files must be deleted and delete them on time.

Your rights, and how to use them

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to: tell you what we hold about you and give you a copy; correct it; delete it; limit how we use it; give it to you in a portable form; stop using it for a purpose you object to; and withdraw consent you gave (for example to the newsletter).

To make a request, email yirehcareers@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request". We reply within one month and it is free. We may ask you to confirm it is you, using details we already hold such as your order email or order number. If your request is complex we may take up to two further months, and we will tell you within the first month. If we cannot do what you ask, we will explain why.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handled your information, tell us first at yirehcareers@gmail.com. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and respond properly. You can also complain to your data protection regulator: your national data protection authority (for example, the Information Commissioner's Office for people in the UK, or the authority in your EU country).

Newsletter

We only email you if you signed up. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and it stops all our emails. Want only the job email? Reply “jobs only” to any tips email and we will stop the tips. Our sign-up form does not send a confirmation email: if you sign up, you are on the list. If you have unsubscribed, signing up again with the same address will not add you back automatically; email us and we will help.

Website statistics and cookies

This site does not set cookies and does not store anything in your browser. It counts visits and clicks with GoatCounter, which does not store your IP address or build a profile of you. We host our fonts ourselves, so your browser does not contact font providers. Our hosting provider keeps ordinary server logs. Links to employers' job pages and to WhatsApp take you to other sites with their own privacy policies.

Children

Our services are for adults. You must be 18 or older to buy a service from us.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell clients who are using the service. Our refund and cancellation terms are on the services page.
