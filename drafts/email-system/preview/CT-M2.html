A LinkedIn headline that says more

Hi Amara,

By default, LinkedIn fills your headline with your job title. You have room for more.

Try this shape: what you do, who you do it for, and one thing that sets you apart. For example: “Customer support specialist for SaaS teams · fluent in English and Spanish”.

Then look at it on your phone, where long headlines get cut short, and make sure the important part comes first.
