# Decision review: refund wording, and the Executive offer for clients already at 500+ connections

Status: PROPOSED. Nothing in this file is published. KT's approval is needed for any wording below.

## 1. Refund policy

### KT's wish
"Imply" a no-refund policy.

### What comparable services do (read 17 Oct 2026)
- Resume Target: full refund before the discovery interview (less a $75 fee with under 24 hours' notice); no refunds after it because work starts immediately; 14 days of unlimited revisions and a 90-day interview guarantee instead. https://www.resumetarget.com/refund-policy.php
- ProResume (UK): full refund within 14 days if work has not begun; 50% to 80% (by package) after work begins but before the first draft; 0% to 25% after the first draft; none after revisions begin; refund only for quality failures left after the full revision process, non-delivery, duplicate payment or technical error; states that UK statutory rights are not affected. https://www.proresume.co.uk/refund-policy
- Majestic Resumes: the guarantee is a free rewrite, not a refund. https://majesticresumes.com/refund-policy/
- SeekingSuccess: "refunds are not possible. There are no exceptions" (search-result excerpt; the page itself was not opened). https://www.seekingsuccess.com/terms-of-service
- Pattern: nearly all use stages (full refund before work starts, little or none after), revisions as the remedy, and a rewrite or partial refund when the service fails. A flat "no refunds, no exceptions" is the outlier.

### Law (summary of official sources, not legal advice)
- EU: consumers who buy a service online have a 14-day right to withdraw without giving a reason, counted from when the contract is made. https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/returns/index_en.htm
- UK: the same 14-day cooling-off period for services arranged online; Citizens Advice describes it. https://www.citizensadvice.org.uk/consumer/changed-your-mind/cancelling-a-service-youve-arranged/
- Common practice (see ProResume above): if the client expressly asks you to start work inside the 14 days, they can still cancel but pay for what has been done; once the service is fully performed with that agreement, the right to cancel is lost. I did not verify the exact rules per country; they differ.
- Your readers are worldwide, so some are consumers in the EU/UK. A blanket no-refund term does not remove rights that the law gives them, and ambiguous wording is usually read against the seller.

### Assessment
"Implying" no refunds is weaker than saying it plainly: it invites disputes and chargeback-style complaints, and it does not remove legal rights. A clear, staged policy gets almost the same business result (no refund for change of mind once work has started) and is honest, which fits the trust the brand depends on. It is also what the services page already says, with one gap: it does not say plainly what happens after work starts.

### Proposed wording (replaces the 3rd bullet under "Refunds and cancellation")
> Once work has started, we do not refund for a change of mind. If we fail to deliver what was agreed, or the final documents do not match what was agreed after your included revisions, we will put it right, and if we cannot, we will refund the part not delivered. Nothing here limits any rights you have under the law where you live.

### Proposed consent step (cooling-off, for EU/UK buyers)
Send, in the payment-received WhatsApp message (message 7): "By sending me your documents you ask me to start work straight away." This records the client's express request to start. KT to confirm; a qualified adviser should check it for the countries you sell to.

### Not changed
The existing site wording stays as published (full refund before onboarding is complete).

## 2. Executive package for clients who already have 500+ connections

### Facts
- LinkedIn allows roughly 100 connection requests per week for most accounts (secondary sources; LinkedIn does not publish a fixed number and accounts can be limited earlier). https://www.joinvalley.co/blog/linkedin-invitation-limit-in-2025-weekly-limits-more
- Free LinkedIn members can add a personal note to only about five connection requests per month. https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a550555
- LinkedIn's rules prohibit bots and third-party automation on accounts, and spam-style requests; accounts can be restricted. Manual, targeted outreach (as the page says) is the safe approach. https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement
- I found no reliable published acceptance rate. It varies widely with how well targeted the profile and audience are. I will not state one.

### Maths (an estimate, not a promise)
About 100 requests a week over 30 days is about 430 requests. If a third are accepted, that is roughly 140 new connections. A target of 100 new connections is therefore reachable in about 30 days for most profiles.
For a client starting at 24, reaching 500 needs about 476 new connections: about 16 weeks at those rates. That is why the page says connection building continues past day 30. It is an open-ended commitment for $99, and a cost to consider.

### Recommendation
For clients who already have 500+ connections: "Our target is 100 new, relevant connections of the types you specify. We send targeted requests manually. The 30 days are the initial window; if the target has not been reached by day 30, we continue until it is, subject to the agreed scope. We cannot promise how many people accept."
This mirrors the 500+ wording, uses the same continue-until-reached mechanism, and fits the 30-day service. A number other than 100 is KT's call.

### Risk to decide on
The open-ended "until reached" promise has no end date for low-start clients. Options: keep as is; cap at a long period (for example 120 days) after which the service closes and is reported; or limit the open-ended promise to clients starting above a minimum. Not changed; KT to decide.

## Update 17 Oct 2026 (later): refund wording approved; Executive economics and flexible wording

### Approved and published
KT approved the refund wording above ("yes to the wording"). It is on the services page (terms and FAQ), and the cooling-off line is in WhatsApp message 7.

### The real issue KT raised: the amount of work for $99
My estimate of the hours in the Executive package (NOT measured; KT should replace these with real figures):
- CV 2-3 h, cover letter 1 h, LinkedIn profile 1.5-2 h, branding strategy 2 h
- 12 posts at about 40 min each = 8 h
- Manual connection building at about 100 requests a week: about 1.5-2 h a week, so about 7 h over the first 30 days
- Three revision rounds about 3 h; client communication about 3 h
- Total for the 30 days: about 28-30 h, which is roughly $3 an hour before payment and exchange-rate costs.
- A client who starts with about 24 connections needs about 476 new ones: roughly 12 more weeks of connection building (about 24 h more). That is about 54 h in total, roughly $2 an hour.
Price signal from the market (ProResume, UK, scraped 17 Oct 2026): its Executive package is GBP 149 for CV writing only. The package here includes a CV, cover letter, LinkedIn profile, branding strategy, 12 posts and connection building for $99.

### Assessment
Flexible wording removes the open-ended risk, but it does not fix the price: even a bounded 30 days is about 30 hours of work. Options:
1. Raise the Executive price (for example to $149 or $199) with the same scope.
2. Keep $99 and cut scope (for example fewer posts, effort-based connection building only).
3. Keep Executive as documents, branding and 12 posts, and sell connection building as a separate 30-day add-on.
4. Keep $99, bound the promise as below, and sell continuation after day 30.
Recommendation: bound the promise now (this is free and protects margin), then decide between options 1 and 3 once KT has timed the real hours on the first two clients. A change in price needs KT's explicit approval.
