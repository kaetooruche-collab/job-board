# Decision review: how clients pay KT (North Cyprus resident)

Status: PROPOSED, awaiting KT's choice. Nothing has been changed on the site or in any account.

## Constraint
KT lives in North Cyprus. Many platforms see the connection as Turkey and do not list North Cyprus as a country. Payments are to go to a personal account KT opens himself. The company KAYZ Global Ltd could not be found in the UK Companies House search; where it is registered is unconfirmed.

## Evidence (read 2026-10-15; secondary sources are marked)
- Payoneer "Request a payment": a receiver can send a payment request that the payer settles by card (3% fee), ACH, bank transfer, PayPal or Payoneer balance. https://www.payoneer.com/get-paid-by-clients/
- Payoneer is available in Turkey; freelancers receive USD and withdraw to local banks (secondary source: StartGlobal, "last verified 2026-06-10"). Whether Payoneer accepts a North Cyprus address is NOT verified.
- Wise: USD account details are not available to an address in Türkiye. https://wise.com/help/articles/2810318/can-i-get-usd-account-details
- PayPal: ceased operating in Turkey in 2016 (secondary sources). Payhip's own PayPal/Stripe/Iyzico integrations therefore do not obviously work for KT. Stripe's supported list has "Cyprus" (the Republic) but not North Cyprus. https://stripe.com/global
- Lemon Squeezy docs list Turkey for bank payouts; Lemon Squeezy is moving to Stripe Managed Payments in 35+ countries (secondary: ceaksan.com). Polar.sh: pays out via Stripe Connect to about 195 countries incl. Türkiye (secondary). Paddle's unsupported list does not include Turkey (https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/which-countries-are-supported-by-paddle) but needs business verification. None of these has been checked against a North Cyprus address.

## Options
A. Payoneer personal/freelancer account + "Request a payment" per order. Fits the WhatsApp-negotiation flow (send a link after agreeing). 3% on card payments. Manual per order. Risk: sign-up may not accept a North Cyprus address; KT must test the country list.
B. Merchant-of-record checkout (Polar, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy): automatic checkout, tax handled by the platform, payout to a bank. Risks: payout bank must accept SWIFT into KT's bank; business verification; address acceptance unverified; higher fees; possible switch to Stripe Managed Payments rules.
C. US LLC (about $400 plus yearly costs) with Mercury/Stripe. Most robust for cards; contradicts KT's wish for a personal account; tax advice needed.
D. Stablecoin (USDT) as an extra rail only. Client friction, compliance and local-law questions.

## Rules
- Use truthful identity and address in every sign-up. Do not enter another country to pass a form: it can freeze funds. If the country list lacks North Cyprus, ask the provider's support which option applies to residents there, and keep the reply.
- This is not tax advice. KT should check local tax obligations for the income.

## Recommendation
A now (works with zero site changes: the Buy buttons already fall back to WhatsApp), test B in parallel only if volume grows. Drop Payhip unless a gateway works.
