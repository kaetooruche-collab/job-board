# Tone review: job board and career services pages (KT, 25 Oct 2026)

KT's brief: some wording is too casual, and in places the tone or framing undersells the services. Keep what makes Yireh Careers personal and different; fix what makes it sound smaller than it is.

## Rule used
Keep warmth where it is the brand (the "How we verify" voice, honest eligibility labels, "Start with a free check"). Change wording that (a) apologises for the service ("small, focused", "small extras", "just the CV", "straightforward, affordable"), (b) leads with a negative ("No online payment", "What we do not promise"), (c) repeats the same disclaimer next to each claim, or (d) makes a claim we cannot back ("job seekers worldwide who trust Yireh Careers").

| Where | Before | After |
|---|---|---|

## Deliberately not changed
Refund, cancellation and personal-information wording (KT-decided terms); the "How we verify" page and trust rules; eligibility and "check each employer's rules" wording (project copy rule); the fictional before/after examples and their "not a client result" labels; the Executive connection-aim explanation (it is the specific caveat for a numeric aim).

## Still open
