# Decision review: name of the paid services ("Kaeto Career Services")

Status: OPEN, needs KT's decision. Raised by KT on 23 Oct 2026 ("I am biased as it is my name... I need pros and cons").
Why it is time-sensitive: the name will appear on the production Whop business, on every buyer receipt, in the card statement descriptor, and on the Whop checkout. It is cheapest to change now: no live Whop account, no customers, no testimonials. Also the board name itself is still a placeholder ("TrueRemote" / `PROJECT_NAME`, CLAUDE.md), so the two names should be decided together.

## Options
A. Keep "Kaeto Career Services" (current). Board stays TrueRemote or its final name.
B. One brand: "[Board name] Career Services", same umbrella as the job board.
C. A new separate brand for the services.
D. Hybrid: services under the board name, with a founder byline ("by Kaeto") on the services page.

## What the evidence in this project shows (real, not opinion)
- Buyers arrive from the job board (nav says "TrueRemote"), then Whop checkout, the receipt email and the bank statement all say "Kaeto Career Services" / "WHOP*KAETO CAREER SERV" (seen in KT's own sandbox purchase). The name changes between the page and the receipt. Whop's own docs say unfamiliar charges cause "I don't recognize this charge" disputes, and a dispute costs $15 plus the amount (see the 19 Oct decision note).
- No testimonials or reviews exist yet, so the name carries no earned reputation that would be lost by changing it.
- Privacy page and services page already explain the legal entity: "KAYZ Global Ltd, trading as Kaeto Career Services".

## Pros and cons
| | Keep "Kaeto Career Services" (A) | Board-brand name (B/D) |
|---|---|---|
| Trust and continuity | Personal accountability; a real person behind it. But a different name from the site that sent the buyer. | One name from job listing to checkout to receipt to bank statement: fewer unrecognized-charge disputes, no "who is this?" moment. |
| Traffic and trust transfer | Board's trust has to be borrowed through a disclosure line. | The board's "verified, free" reputation attaches directly to the service. |
| Brand building | Two brands to build (board and services), testimonials split. | One brand, one set of reviews, one search term. |
| Name quality | Distinctive and probably ownable, no hits found in a basic web search (not a trademark check). Risk: reads and sounds like "keto" (the diet), easy to misspell ("Kaito", "Keato"), says nothing about CVs. | Depends on the final board name, which is not chosen yet. |
| Personal fit | Strong for a founder-led, 1:1 WhatsApp service; useful for the LinkedIn Growth/Executive offers where KT's own marketing credibility is the product. | Founder byline can still carry that. |
| Future | Tied to one person: harder to sell, hand over, or add other writers. | Easier to scale or sell as a business. |
| Switching cost | Low now, rising with every receipt, review and link. | n/a |
| Legal | Trading name needs to be registered as such where KAYZ Global Ltd is registered (accountant or registered agent to confirm; not checked). | Same check. |

## Recommendation (Claude's, for KT to accept or reject)
Choose D: decide the umbrella name for the board first (it is a placeholder anyway), then call the services "[Board name] Career Services" with a visible "by Kaeto" founder byline. Reason: all paid traffic comes from the board, one name end to end protects conversion and lowers dispute risk, and the cheapest moment to switch is before the production Whop business exists. Keep "Kaeto" as the personal/founder name where trust in a person matters (services page, WhatsApp greeting, LinkedIn).
Counter-view that would change the recommendation: if KT intends to build a personal brand (speaking, content, a following) beyond this site, A or D with the personal name more prominent is right, and "Kaeto" should then be the thing the board points to.
