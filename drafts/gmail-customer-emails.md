# Gmail templates for service emails (Yireh Careers)

Generated by `drafts/email-system/build_library.py` from `scripts/email_data.py`. Do not edit by hand. Replaces the first pack (TR-01 to TR-07).

**Why Gmail and not Brevo:** orders happen on WhatsApp, so Brevo never learns that someone paid; documents travel as attachments in the same thread; and these emails carry no promotion, so they need no marketing consent. They are sent by hand from yirehcareers@gmail.com.

**Easier way (added 25 Oct 2026): use the formatted version at https://yirehcareers.vercel.app/mail** (unlisted). It has the same emails in the Yireh Careers letter design with the logo, fills in the first name and package for you, and copies them ready to paste into Gmail. This plain-text pack stays as the fallback and as the review copy.

**One-time setup (on a computer):** Gmail, Settings (gear), See all settings, Advanced, Templates: Enable, Save. For each email below: write a new message, paste the subject and body, three dots at the bottom right, Templates, Save draft as template, Save as new template, and use the name shown. To use one: new message, three dots, Templates, pick it, fill in the [brackets], send.

Every email ends with the same two lines (add them once to your Gmail signature):

```
Best,
The Yireh Careers team
Questions? Reply here, or message us on WhatsApp: +90 533 822 5854
```
