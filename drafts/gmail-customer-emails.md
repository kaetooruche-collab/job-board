# Gmail replies for customer emails (Yireh Careers)

Generated by `drafts/emails/build_emails.py` from the same copy as the Brevo templates. Do not edit by hand.

**Why Gmail and not Brevo for these:** orders happen on WhatsApp, so Brevo never learns that a customer paid or finished onboarding; Brevo cannot attach each customer's documents; Brevo cannot send authenticated mail from a Gmail address; and keeping customers out of Brevo keeps their details in fewer places. Gmail sends from the real mailbox and the customer's reply stays in the same thread as their documents.

**One-time setup in Gmail (on a computer):** Settings (gear), See all settings, Advanced, Templates: Enable, Save. Then for each email below: write a new message, paste the subject and body, tap the three dots at the bottom right, Templates, Save draft as template, Save as new template, and give it the name shown. To use one: new message, three dots, Templates, pick it, fill in the [brackets], send.

## TR-01 Order received and onboarding

When: Send when you have confirmed the customer's payment. Attach nothing.

Template name in Gmail: `TR-01 Order received and onboarding`

Subject:

```
Thanks, here's how we get started
```

Body:

```
Hi [First name],

We've got your [Package] order. To get going, just reply to this email with:

- your current CV (Word, PDF or plain text), and your LinkedIn link if you have one
- the kind of roles you're going for, and the countries you can work in
- any job postings you like (optional)

That's it. You don't need to send ID or bank details. Email works best for documents, so they stay private and in one place. WhatsApp is perfect for questions.

Your delivery time starts once we have everything, and we'll confirm your dates as soon as we do.

If you don't see our emails later on, take a quick look in Promotions or Spam.

Thanks,
The Yireh Careers team

Questions? Reply here, or message us on WhatsApp: +90 533 822 5854
```
