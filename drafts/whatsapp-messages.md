# WhatsApp message library (True Remote · Kaeto Career Services)

Purpose: every stage of an order is handled on WhatsApp, with documents only by email. Each message is short, plain and polite, and says what happens next. Use the **WhatsApp Business app** (already linked to +90 533 822 5854) so these can be saved as quick replies (type "/" and a shortcut) and as the greeting and away messages. Check the feature names in your app, as they can change.

Conventions: `[Square brackets]` = fill in. `*bold*` is WhatsApp bold. Prices, timings and terms below match the services page; do not change them in a chat without updating the page first.

## 0. Profile (set once)
Business name: True Remote · Kaeto Career Services. Category: Professional services. Description: "CVs, cover letters and LinkedIn profiles, written for you." Email: trueremote.jobs@gmail.com. Website: the services page.

## 1. Pre-filled message (already on the website buttons)
> Hello, I would like to order the *Essential* package ($29). Could you please confirm the next steps?

(The same wording is used for Professional ($49) and Executive ($99).)

## 2. Greeting (automatic, first message from a new contact)
> Hello, and welcome to True Remote by Kaeto Career Services. Thank you for your message.
> To help us reply properly, please tell us which package you are interested in (Essential $29, Professional $49 or Executive $99) and the kind of roles you are applying for. We will reply as soon as we can.

## 3. Away message (automatic, outside your hours)
> Thank you for your message. We are currently away and will reply when we are back. You can see the packages and what they include at [services page link].

## 4. Confirm the package and ask for the currency
Shortcut `/package`
> Hello [First name], thank you for choosing the *[Package]* package ($[price]).
>
> *What is included:* [see inclusions below]
> *Timing:* [timing]. The timing starts when onboarding is complete, which means once we have everything we need from you.
>
> Which would you prefer for payment: bank transfer in USD, EUR, GBP or TL, or cryptocurrency? I will send the details as soon as you tell me.

Inclusions to paste:
- Essential: CV creation or improvement, tailored to your industry and target roles; ATS-optimised; Word and PDF; up to 2 revision rounds. Timing: 5 working days.
- Professional: everything in Essential, plus a targeted cover letter and LinkedIn profile setup; up to 3 revision rounds. Timing: 5 working days.
- Executive: everything in Professional, plus a personal branding strategy, manual targeted LinkedIn connection building (target 500+ total connections, continuing past day 30 until reached), 12 LinkedIn posts written and published over 30 days, and the 30-day LinkedIn growth service. Timing: CV and cover letter within 2 working days; LinkedIn profile setup within 5 working days; the 30-day service period starts when onboarding is complete.

## 7. Payment received: what happens next
Shortcut `/received`
> Payment received. Thank you, [First name]. Your order is confirmed.
>
> *Next step:* I have emailed you from trueremote.jobs@gmail.com with simple instructions. Please reply to that email with your CV and any other documents. Documents go by email only, please not by WhatsApp.

>
