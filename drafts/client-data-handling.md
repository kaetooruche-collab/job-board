# Client data handling: how to keep and delete client documents (email-based)

Decision (KT): conversations and negotiation happen on WhatsApp; **all documents travel by email only**. There is no storage platform. We promise deletion within 90 days of completion. This replaces the earlier Proton Drive plan.

## 1. Honest limits to tell clients (already in the onboarding email)
- Email is not end-to-end encrypted. Clients are asked to send only what the CV or profile needs, and never passport or ID numbers, bank details or passwords.
- WhatsApp is for chatting only. If a client sends a document on WhatsApp anyway, reply asking them to resend by email, then delete the WhatsApp copy.

## 2. Set-up (once)
1. Use the business inbox trueremote.jobs@gmail.com (not a personal inbox). Turn on 2-step verification and keep the password in a password manager.
2. Create one Gmail **label per client** named `Client – <order number>` (the order number from Payhip, not the full name). Apply it to every email in the thread.
3. Create a folder `Clients` on the laptop with one subfolder per order number for working copies. Nothing is kept on a phone.
4. Keep the log `client-data-log-template.csv` (order number, package, date received, date onboarding complete, date delivered, date completed, delete-by date, deleted on).

## 3. During the order
- Download attachments into the order's subfolder. Do the work there.
- Send deliverables by email attachment, using templates TR-03 and TR-04. Ask the client to keep their own copy.
- Do not forward client documents to other people or paste them into third-party tools unless the client has been told about it.

## 4. Completion and deletion (promise: within 90 days; aim for day 45)
Send template TR-05 on completion and set the delete-by date.
1. Delete the order subfolder from the laptop and empty the recycle bin; delete anything in Downloads.
2. In Gmail: open the client label, delete every thread, then empty **Trash**, check **Sent** and **Drafts** for stray copies, and delete the label.
3. WhatsApp: delete the chat media (documents, photos) and, if the client agrees, the chat. Check linked devices.
4. Not deleted by us: Payhip order record and the payment provider's records (they follow their own retention). The newsletter contact in Brevo stays only if the client opted in.
5. Record the deletion date in the log.

## 5. When someone asks for their data (access, correction, deletion, "stop emailing me")
- Reply **within one month**, free of charge (template TR-06 acknowledges). Ask for proof only if reasonable; use the email or order number you already hold. Do not ask for ID unless there is no other way.
- Search: WhatsApp (name and number, including linked devices), Gmail (address and name, including Sent, Trash and Drafts), the laptop folder, Payhip (customer record), Brevo (contact).
- Do what they asked: send a copy, correct, delete, or stop emailing. For deletion also remove them from Brevo and say honestly what must be kept (payment records).
- Record the dates in the log.

## 6. If something goes wrong (wrong recipient, lost laptop, hacked inbox)
- Write down what happened, when, and which clients are affected.
- Tell the affected clients quickly and plainly. Reporting duties to the regulator can apply (the UK GDPR / GDPR allow 72 hours from becoming aware when there is a risk to people): ask for advice from a data-protection professional before deciding not to report.
- Change the inbox password, turn on 2-step verification and review the account's recent activity.

## 7. Still to settle before the privacy policy goes live
- Legal name and location to show (Brevo shows KAYZ Global Ltd, Girne).
- A privacy contact email, and whether trueremote.jobs@gmail.com is it.
- Whether any AI tool is used on client documents (if yes, it must be disclosed).
- Gmail account type (personal or Workspace) and invoice retention period.
- Minimum age for clients; double confirmation for the newsletter.

## 8. AI writing tools (disclosed in the privacy policy and on the services page)
KT uses AI tools only to help improve the wording of documents. Because client text may contain personal data, follow these rules:
- Turn off any setting that lets the provider train its models on your content (look for "improve the model for everyone" or similar in the tool's data controls) and check the provider's current terms.
- Where possible, paste only the text that needs rewording, without the client's name, email, phone number, address or other direct identifiers, and add them back afterwards.
- Never paste passport/ID numbers, bank details or passwords (clients are told not to send them).
- Delete the conversations that contain client text from the AI tool when the order is completed (part of the 90-day deletion routine).
- If the tool changes its terms or you switch tools, update the privacy policy.
