# CV tailoring rules (every job)
1. Start from the master CV in `private/master-cv.*`. Never add a skill, tool, project or result that is not true.
2. Re-order, do not rewrite history: put the experience that matches this posting first (placement, dissertation, module, project, volunteering).
3. Use the posting's own words where they are true for you (the "CV keywords" column). Applicant tracking systems match on them.
4. Summary (3 lines): who you are (MPharm 2024), the exact role family, and two true proofs that match the posting.
5. One page for early career, two at most. Plain format (no tables or text boxes in the Word file; ATS-friendly). Save as PDF and Word.
6. Licence honesty: say "MPharm (2024)" and the registration status plainly. Never imply you are a registered pharmacist.
7. Check dates, titles and spellings against the master CV before sending.
8. File name: `Firstname-Lastname-CV-<Company>-<Role>.pdf`.
