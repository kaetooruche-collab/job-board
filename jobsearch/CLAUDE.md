# KT's personal pharmacy job search (separate project)

Start with `README.md`, then `HANDOFF.md` and `TODO.md`. This folder is private: excluded from the website by `../.vercelignore`.

Rules:
- Only jobs posted within 7 days; each posting opened and read in full; dates from the employer's own data.
- Rate fit honestly using the rating standard v2 in `AGENT_BRIEF.md`; do not pad the list to reach a number.
- Contacts only if the employer published them; never guess or scrape emails.
- Never invent experience, results or qualifications on a CV or letter; use only KT's CV and answers.
- Never open or scrape LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter or similar (their terms forbid it). KT searches them by hand and pastes links.
- Nothing is emailed or submitted for KT without KT's explicit approval. Draft only.
- Do not use the job board's classifier (`../scripts/classify.py`) here, and do not change job-board files from this chat.
- KT's master CV and generated documents go in `private/` (private repo, never deployed).
- Report real numbers (for example "9 of 10 fully correct"), never "looks fine".
