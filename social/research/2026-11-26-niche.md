# The remote-job niche on TikTok and Instagram (26 Nov 2026)

**Question:** what do remote-job and job-search accounts that reach Africa and Asia post, how do they hook, and what gets watched and saved?
**Method:** Firecrawl (ScrapeCreators provider) keyword searches on 26 Nov 2026: TikTok "remote jobs for Africans", "remote job scam warning", "remote jobs India work from home international" (30 videos each, last 3 months, relevance order); Instagram Reels "remote jobs hiring worldwide", "remote jobs Nigeria Kenya Philippines" (10 each, last month). Region-proxied TikTok searches (NG, PH, KE) returned nothing. Cost: about 8 Firecrawl credits. Numbers are as returned on 26 Nov.

**Labels:** **[Established]** = read in the data above; **[Reported]** = a secondary source; **[Hypothesis]** = our reading, untested.
**Limits:** one search sample of about 110 posts, ranked by the platforms' relevance, so it over-represents hits; no follower counts; viewer countries are unknown (a creator's topic is not proof of where viewers live); captions only, the videos were not watched.

## 2. What it means for us
1. **Lists and checklists are saved.** [Established for these posts] Save rates (saves ÷ views): @aminahmars 9.8%, @heyitsmesade 7.2%, @mal_wfh 6.2%, @deborahdosunmuofficial 5.5%, @repella1 4.5%. Scam stories get views but few saves (@officialtyrrancemill 0.5%, CBC 0.6%). [Hypothesis] Our role carousels and tip checklists are "save" formats; judge them on saves and shares, not likes.
2. **Africa-facing creators get reach with a face and a direct address** ("Africans, …", "from Africa", a named country), and long explainers (2-4 minutes) reach 100,000+ there. [Hypothesis] We cannot copy the country address (KT's rule: no country named) — our version is "wherever you live", which is weaker in the hook and must be made up with a specific, checkable fact (a number, a real role).
3. **The country filter is not unique.** [Established] A creator already sells Himalayas on "filter by country to see only the roles Nigerians [can apply to]". So we never claim to be the only one; our difference is the evidence on every card (where you can apply from in the employer's words, the posting date, a link to the original posting, no account, never a fee), and roles open beyond one country posted as they are.
4. **Instagram reach in this niche is a fraction of TikTok's** (same video: 89,113 TikTok views vs 8,622 Instagram). [Established for one creator; Hypothesis in general] Expect TikTok to lead early numbers; do not judge Instagram by the same bar.
5. **The scam script is common in the niche** (comment-for-the-link, "fill out the link to receive details", course upsells). [Established that it appears] Our rule stands: the CTA is a visible link to the board, never "comment" or "DM"; `check_post.py` blocks it.
6. **Series help** ("DAY 3/30 remote job boards in 30 days"). [Hypothesis] A recurring named slot ("This week's roles open beyond one country") may build return viewers; test after week 2.
