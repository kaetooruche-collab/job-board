## 2. What it means for us
1. **Our pain is a proven hook in the biggest market.** [Established] "remote should ACTUALLY be remote 😭" reached 706,690 views. "Hiring in multiple countries" and "todas contratan desde Latinoamérica" (all hire from Latin America) are recurring hooks. Our angle, where you can actually apply from, is not niche outside the US.
2. **"Roles you can apply for this week" is a working format in the UK** (@vabynura: about 200,000-300,000 views, saves around 5-6%). [Established] It's the same as our Wednesday and Saturday carousels, with pay stated where the posting gives it. Keep pay on the card.
3. **"Rating the platforms" is the most-saved format in every region:** @mal_wfh (186,257 saves, 7.9%), @isa_en_remoto in Spanish (6.0%). [Hypothesis] A "how to tell if a remote job site is worth your time" tip carousel (no names, criteria only: shows where you can apply from, shows the posting date, links the original posting, never charges) fits our rules and this format. Added as a future tip; naming competitors needs KT's yes.
4. **Latin America works in Spanish and Portuguese.** [Established] The Latin American hits are in Spanish or Portuguese, and "paid in dollars" (*en dólares*) is the desire. [Hypothesis] English posts will reach only English-reading Latin American professionals. Spanish captions or subtitles are a later test and need KT's decision (copy, checking and spelling rules are English-only today).
5. **Deadlines work as urgency, honestly.** [Established for one post] "If you want a new job by January, start applying this month" (192,102 views) uses a real seasonal fact. A December post can do the same without overpromising.
6. **The scam-shaped CTA appears in the UK too** ("Dm me REMOTE"). [Established] Our link-in-bio rule stands.
