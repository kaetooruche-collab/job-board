## 3. One slot that suits both halves
| UTC | Ghana | Lagos | Johannesburg | Nairobi | Karachi | Delhi | Dhaka | Jakarta | Manila |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **12:00** | 12:00 | 13:00 | 14:00 | 15:00 | 17:00 | 17:30 | 18:00 | 19:00 | 20:00 |
| 15:00 | 15:00 | 16:00 | 17:00 | 18:00 | 20:00 | 20:30 | 21:00 | 22:00 | 23:00 |
| 09:00 | 09:00 | 10:00 | 11:00 | 12:00 | 14:00 | 14:30 | 15:00 | 16:00 | 17:00 |

**12:00 UTC** lands in Africa's early afternoon (Sprout's window) and South and South-East Asia's evening (Buffer's window). Nothing else covers both. [Hypothesis]

## 4. Decision for the first two weeks
- **Every post at 12:00 UTC**, Tuesday to Saturday plus Monday; no Sunday posts (Sprout's worst day; Buffer disagrees, so Sunday is a later test, not a default).
- Hold the time fixed for two weeks so it does not muddy the hook and format tests.
- **Week 3 test:** 12:00 vs 15:00 UTC for the same post type (15:00 favours Africa's late afternoon and Asia's late evening), once there is a baseline.
- **Use our own data as soon as it exists:** TikTok Studio → Analytics → Followers shows when followers are active and where viewers are; Instagram Insights shows follower locations and active hours (both need a minimum number of followers; the exact threshold was not checked).
