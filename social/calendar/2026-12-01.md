## Mon 07 Dec: Tip: three signs of a job scam (`w49-p6`)

- **Time:** 15:00 UTC (Mexico City 09:00 · Toronto 10:00 · São Paulo 12:00 · London 15:00 · Berlin 16:00 · Lagos 16:00 · Nairobi 18:00 · Delhi 20:30 · Manila 23:00)
- **Platforms:** instagram, tiktok, facebook · **Pillar:** tips · **Format:** carousel
- **Hook:** A real employer won't ask you to pay to get a job.
- **Variable tested:** Topic: safety (our audience's top fear) against Thursday's eligibility tip; compare saves and shares.
- **Visuals:** Five text slides: cover, three signs, closing slide.

**Slides:**

1. (cover) Stay safe — A real employer won't ask you to pay — Three warning signs to know before you share any details.
2. (tip) A fee to apply or train — Registration fees, training kits or equipment you must buy first are a warning sign.
3. (tip) Interviews only by text — An interview held only by text on Telegram or WhatsApp is a common scam pattern.
4. (tip) A role you can't find — Look for the job on the company's careers site before you send anything.
5. (cta) How our board works — We never charge you to apply — Every job on it opens its original posting, with the date it was posted.
