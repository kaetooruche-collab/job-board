# Content pillars (27 Nov 2026)

Fifteen topics, grouped into five families so the week stays balanced. Shares are a starting mix, adjusted by the Friday review. Every post records its pillar, funnel stage and goal metric.

| Family (share of a 6-post week) | Pillars | Typical formats | Primary goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Find jobs you can apply for** (2 posts) | 1 Remote job discovery · 2 Geographic eligibility and worldwide hiring · 11 Newly discovered opportunities | roles carousel (live feed, verified against employer data), single-role screen video, Story with link | board visits, saves |
| **B. Search smarter** (1-2 posts) | 3 Remote job-search education · 6 Application mistakes · 9 Job-market misconceptions · 10 Website demonstrations | screen demo video, myth/correction carousel, static | saves, sends |
| **C. Get job-ready** (1 post) | 4 CV and ATS advice · 5 LinkedIn optimisation · 7 Interview preparation · 8 Career development | presenter video, tip carousel | saves, profile visits |
| **D. Stay safe and sane** (1 post) | scam signs (part of 3 and 9) · 15 Light-hearted, relatable job-search content | presenter video, static, question post | sends, comments |
| **E. Us and the community** (≤1 post) | 12 Yireh Careers brand awareness · 13 Free resources and services · 14 Community questions and discussions | question post, free CV check post (service max 1 in 6) | comments, free-check requests |

## Rules per pillar
- **1, 2, 11 (jobs):** roles re-opened and matched to the employer's own data on the post day (`verify-open.js`, `verify_source.py`); eligibility in the employer's words; no country named; numbers dated. "Newly discovered" means posted in the last 14 days by the employer's date, never "new to us" presented as new.
- **3, 6, 9, 10 (search smarter):** show, don't tell; screen recordings of the real board; every myth correction cites what's on the board or a public source.
- **4, 5, 7, 8 (job-ready):** practical and specific; no promise of interviews or offers; the CV service is not mentioned in these unless it's the week's one service post. Claims about ATS behaviour cite a source or are framed as "what we check".
- **Scams and relatable (D):** serious tone for scams; humour only for harmless absurdities (never mocking people for being rejected).
- **12-14 (E):** the free CV check first; paid packages only with current prices from `site-config.js`; community questions must be ones we'll actually read and answer (KT handles comments and DMs).
