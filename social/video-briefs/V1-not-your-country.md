# V1: "Remote… but not for you?"
Status: **rendered and composited 27 Nov; waiting for KT to listen** (KT approved script, swaps and credits 27 Nov) · Presenter: Amara · Length target: 25-30 s · Platforms: TikTok, Instagram Reel, Facebook (screen-only cut unless KT ticks "AI info")

1. **Objective:** awareness → profile visits and board visits. Goal metric: 2-second hold and completion (TikTok Studio), profile visits.
2. **Audience:** people outside the US who keep finding "remote" jobs they can't apply for, at any level.
3. **Audience insight:** "fully remote, and then in the text it's either US only, or specific state/city" (r/RemoteJobs); "No, a remote job doesn't mean you can work from any country you like" (r/expats). Named on three continents (`AUDIENCE_RESEARCH.md`).
4. **Concept:** the moment of discovery, halfway through an application, played straight and recognisable, then one practical habit (check the location line first), then the board as the shortcut. The viewer remembers: *remote tells you where you'll work, not where you can live.*
5. **Emotion:** frustrated (mild, shared) → reflective → reassuring. Not angry, not dramatic.
6. **Opening visual:** Amara mid-sentence, slightly amused, hook text on screen from frame 0.
7. **Opening spoken hook:** "You find a remote job that actually fits."
8. **Spoken script** (77 words, about 29 s at 160 wpm):
   > You find a remote job that actually fits. Right role. Decent pay.
   > You get halfway through the application… and there it is, right at the bottom. "Must be based in" a country that isn't yours.
   > Remote tells you where you'll work. It doesn't tell you where you can live.
   > So check that line first. Before the cover letter. Before anything.
   > On Yai-ray Careers, every job shows where it hires from, or says when the employer doesn't.
   > Link's in the bio.
