Ever skipped a remote job because it said EMEA?
EMEA is Europe, the Middle East and Africa.
APAC is Asia and the Pacific. LATAM is Latin America.
So a job marked EMEA can include you... even when it never names your country.
Some employers narrow it down further in the posting, so give it a quick read.
